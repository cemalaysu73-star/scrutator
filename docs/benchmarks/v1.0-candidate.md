# Scrutator v1.0 candidate: benchmark and gate

Runs a to d and the timing were made on 2026-10-20 between 22:21 and 00:22 (local time), run r5 on 2026-10-21 at
00:49, all from one machine and network. The
harness (`bench/run_bench.py`), the four frozen targets, the 900 s timeout and the competitors (sublist3r 1.0,
dnsrecon 0.10.1, knockpy 9.0.0) are those of `2026-10-17-baseline.md`, `2026-10-17-after-v0.6.md` and

document checks that it did not make them worse.

## Speed

Where Scrutator still loses, ranked, unchanged from v0.7 apart from what is said above:

1. **Time.** Tens of seconds against 3 to 7 for knockpy. The floor is the rate limit of the service that gives network
   owners, plus Python start-up.
2. **Recall on the one real truth set**: 4 of 31 names in zonetransfer.me. 27 names (`vpn`, `owa`, SRV and NAPTR owners
   and so on) are reached by no passive tool tried; that is where a scope-gated zone transfer belongs (planned, active).
3. **Netcraft**: sublist3r's only working engine finds live names nobody else does. Its terms were never evaluated.
4. **Quota-limited sources decide the recall of a day.** HackerTarget's 50 calls a day and CertSpotter's rate limit are
   spent by a few scans from one address. The v0.7 document's 89% and this document's 91% for owasp.org differ by one
   source's availability.
5. **The claimed tier is only as good as DNS at one moment.**
6. **The keyed sources are untested against the real services.**
7. **Pseudo-truth is circular** and dnsrecon never produced a result.
8. **The strongest passive tools were not run** (subfinder, Amass, SpiderFoot, theHarvester; they need an executable or
   a repository download that needs a person's approval).
