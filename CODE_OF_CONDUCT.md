# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to find out what an organisation exposes so that it can be fixed. We want it to
be a place where that work can be discussed calmly.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Give feedback you would be willing to receive: specific, kind, and aimed at the change.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Using the project to target, profile or locate individuals, or asking others to. Requests for help that amount to
  that are declined and may be reported.
- Publishing someone's private information, or reports about an organisation you have no right to assess.
- Sharing exploits, credentials or keys obtained without authorisation.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a
vulnerability**, marking the report "conduct", or, for something that is not sensitive, use GitHub's "Report content"
option on the comment or issue. Maintainers may remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may
suspend or ban people who do. Security problems follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

This policy applies in the project's repositories and in spaces where someone represents the project.
